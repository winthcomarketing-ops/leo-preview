Local Agent Leo

Useful marketing.
Your firm stays in control.

Local Agent Leo is being built for practices that need a clear connection between marketing ideas, firm approval and what actually goes live.

1. Start with verified practice information

Confirm your services, professional registration, firm, website and actual service areas. New connections start read-only. The initial plan focuses on the people your practice can help.

2. Prepare changes you can review

The team prepares content and relevant internal-link changes, with required firm disclosures. Every proposed public version goes to the compliance queue.

3. Record the required approval

Approval belongs to the exact version reviewed. Changed copy, destinations or disclosures need a new review. Joint educational content needs the participating firms’ review.

4. Publish only through an approved connection

Production publishing is enabled only after setup and validation. The goal is a record of the published version, its approval and re-review date.

What is available today?

The early-access request form is open. The client platform is in development. Live integrations, firm onboarding and pilot participation are confirmed individually. No platform feature replaces your firm’s review or your professional responsibilities.
