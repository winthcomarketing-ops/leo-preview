GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you meet Leo.

Can Leo get my practice cited in an AI answer?

Leo helps prepare useful content, clear local facts and website improvements that support discoverability. AI systems decide what to cite. Mentions, links, rankings and new clients are not guaranteed, and live AI citation tracking is not connected.

Is Local Agent Leo available now?

We are preparing an early-access pilot. You can request access today. Live services, connections and availability are confirmed individually before onboarding.

When should I expect results?

Start with a baseline and a practical work plan. Review progress at 30, 60 and 90 days. Search changes can take longer, and rankings or new clients are never guaranteed. Connected reporting helps you see the work and the evidence.

Why do you need my email for a free scan?

Your email links the scan to your prospect record and unlocks the full report in this browser. Marketing messages are optional and require separate consent and email confirmation. The scan does not create a subscription.

How hands-off is Leo?

The goal is to automate routine research, drafting and monitoring after setup. Your firm still reviews public changes, and you approve any charges. Leo is in early access, so connected capabilities are confirmed during onboarding.

Will this work with my existing website?

That is the goal. We assess your website and provider first. Where direct editing is restricted, the workflow is designed to prepare changes for your approved site vendor.

Does Leo publish without my approval?

For financial advisors and CPAs, public changes must go through the required firm review. Approval applies to the exact version that will be published.

Does Leo guarantee rankings or new clients?

No. Search visibility depends on competition, location, relevance and other factors. The aim is better, measurable marketing work and a clearer path to a consultation.

What does it cost?

There is no charge to request early access. Pilot scope and any future pricing will be shared before you commit. No payment or subscription is created by this form.

What about SEO, AEO and GEO?

The plan covers local search optimization, clear answers to customer questions, geographic targeting and useful content for AI search. These are complementary disciplines, not guaranteed shortcuts.
