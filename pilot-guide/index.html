Welcome to your
Leo starting point.

Use this checklist when your pilot access is confirmed. It keeps setup focused and helps you know what is ready, what needs review and what happens next.

1. Confirm your practice

Have your public website, practice name, registration type, firm, services and actual service areas ready. Never enter client account numbers, Social Security numbers or financial records.

2. Establish your firm’s rules

Identify the authorized reviewer and obtain approved disclosures for your exact legal entity and registration. A public website footer is not approval. Review requests and replies remain off until your firm sets its policy.

3. Confirm your website access

Identify your site vendor and who can implement approved changes. If your vendor controls the site, Leo can prepare a handoff. Do not share a website password in chat or email.

4. Connect reporting securely

Connect Search Console from Leo’s connection page using the appropriate Google account and owned property. Rank data has separate setup and owner confirmation. Never paste access tokens or API keys into messages.

5. Save your baseline and activate

Verify the site, keywords, location and device before comparing results. Confirm recurring monitoring is actually activated and check its last successful collection. A saved setup alone does not start background work.

6. Review progress together

Day 30: review the foundation, completed work and pending approvals. Day 60: examine comparable visibility and inquiry evidence. Day 90: decide what to refine or expand. Weekly reporting starts after day 30 once activated. Results can take longer and are not guaranteed.

Keep your next step simple.

Start with your public practice details. Live integrations, firm policy and pilot availability are confirmed separately.
