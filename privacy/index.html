Early-access privacy notice

Updated October 5, 2026. This notice covers the Local Agent Leo early-access website, request form and saved practice setup.

What we collect

If you submit the form, we collect your business email, practice website, practice type, primary city and your permission to contact you about early access. Do not submit investor information, account numbers or other sensitive client records.

Saved plan requests

If you save a proposed plan, we store your business email, the displayed offer and your permission to contact you about that plan. This does not start a subscription, lock a price or enroll you in a marketing campaign. No card information is collected by the save form.

Saved practice setup

If you sign in and save a setup, we store your practice details, self-reported registration, public license reference, firm, services, locations, website preferences and optional disclosure wording. Setup is linked to your signed-in account using a keyed identifier and encrypted before storage. These details prepare onboarding and do not verify your license or activate live services. You can delete your saved setup from your dashboard. Avoid entering client information or secrets.

Why we collect it

We use these details to review pilot interest and contact you about availability and onboarding. Submitting this form does not create a paid subscription. Contact details are encrypted before they are stored in our application database.

Website assessment and follow-up

The scanner requests public website pages and makes scan sessions available for 24 hours. Unlocked report snapshots are retained with the prospect record to support follow-up. It does not log in, submit forms or modify the scanned site. Before starting a scan, we require your email and permission to save the scan details. Your full report is unlocked in the browser without a second email form. We store your email, website, target city, practice type and consent choices as a prospect record. Email addresses are encrypted. Marketing tips are optional and require separate consent and email confirmation. Resend will process delivery if email sending is activated. Unsubscribe links stop future marketing. Starting onboarding does not make you a paying client. Time and price recommendations are estimates, not guarantees or payment authorization.

Google reporting connections

If you choose to connect Google, Leo requests read-only Search Console permission and renewable access. We encrypt access and refresh tokens and bind them to your signed-in Leo account. Leo stores finalized website search clicks, impressions and reporting dates for the website you select. We do not request your Google password, Gmail messages, investor records or permission to publish through this connection. Google data is used to provide your reporting and is not used to train general-purpose AI models, sold or shared for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect from your connections page or revoke permission in your Google account. Disconnecting removes Leo’s stored tokens; saved measurements remain until you delete your practice setup.

SEO drafts and review records

If you save scan findings to the action center, Leo stores encrypted tasks, drafts, version history, firm approval entries and vendor implementation references under your signed-in account. Editing a draft invalidates its prior approval for future handoff while retaining historical records. Approval entries and vendor implementation entries are supplied by the account and are not independently verified through a firm or website integration. Reports may include these recorded statuses, clearly separate from verified completed work. Deleting your practice setup deletes these application records; export records required by your firm before deletion. No direct website publishing is enabled.

Location suggestions

When available, approximate city information supplied by our hosting network is used to suggest a starting location. We do not save your IP address or network coordinates in your practice setup. Only the cities you select and save become your target areas. Network location may be inaccurate, so you can always change it.

Hosting and abuse prevention

The website uses OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare hosting infrastructure. Hosting providers process technical request information to deliver and protect the site. The form uses short-lived, keyed identifiers to limit repeated submissions. We do not use advertising pixels or sell the information submitted through this form.

Your choices

Participation is optional. You can ask us to stop contacting you or delete your early-access details by replying to any early-access message you receive. Saved practice setup can be deleted directly from your dashboard. Full service policies will be provided before live client services are activated.

Retention and access

Early-access information is retained to manage pilot requests until it is deleted or no longer needed for that purpose. It is not exposed through a public list or public read endpoint. Only authorized operators should handle requests. This website does not currently accept client documents or financial account data.
